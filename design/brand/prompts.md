# BB TV — aktualne prompty

Wygenerowano wbudowanym narzędziem `image_gen`; bez API ani CLI. Referencje dostarczone przez użytkownika: Apple Home oraz zestaw Podcasts/Home w `references/`. Pliki referencyjne służą wyłącznie do porównania stylu; nie są eksportowane do aplikacji.

## Ikona

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: BB TV production app icon, square 1024x1024, opaque, unmasked source artwork.
Input images: Image 1 = Apple Home icon, exact material/style reference. Image 2 = Apple Podcasts and Home icons, additional exact style reference. These are style references, not subjects to reproduce.
Primary request: Match the restrained Apple layered icon construction in the provided references as closely as possible, replacing the Home house subject with a simple BB TV television-with-play symbol in blue. Precisely match the Home icon's quiet near-flat dimensionality, filled colored overlapping layers, incredibly fine warm-white edge highlights, hairline darker edges, subtle soft ambient shadows, calm white background and elegant mathematical proportions. The user specifically rejected a photorealistic shiny glass TV render; this MUST look like a real first-party Apple icon in the reference sheet.
Canvas: full-bleed opaque very soft off-white #fafafa to #f3f3f4 background. NO outer rounded-square silhouette, no black surround, no rounded tile floating on another background, no outer margin or drop shadow of an app tile: OS masking will handle the icon's outer corners.
Subject: a centered simple filled blue TV silhouette, wide rounded rectangular screen body with a short central neck and broad rounded low foot, built from clean solid shapes. Front-facing absolutely flat orthographic geometry, no tilted perspective. Main TV body width 70% of the canvas, body height 47%, generous whitespace around the whole mark. Overall TV including foot occupies about 58% canvas height.
Layer construction: outer body a beautiful calm saturated Apple blue #087ef5; inset filled rounded rectangle #44a5ff with a precise thin light upper edge and darker lower edge; inner filled rounded screen #8dccff, with the same restrained layer separation. Center a single small rounded triangular play glyph pointing right, pale icy almost white #f0faff, about 19% of canvas width. Shapes are FILLED and planar, nested like the nested houses of the Home reference. Blue surfaces have only an extremely gentle gradient and very subtle translucency. A tiny physically soft shadow under each nested layer expresses 1-2mm of separation. Keep the outline micro highlights no heavier than those of the supplied Apple Home icon.
Materials: Apple Icon Composer's layered tinted material, soft translucent enamel/glass sheets with restrained micro bevel. The mark should read first as an elegant simple graphic, with depth only in the subtle edges and shadows. Match the reference's softness and polish.
Text: none.
Avoid: bright studio reflections, tubular glass borders, clear transparent TV case, watery/bubbly glass, lens distortion, photorealistic object rendering, hard cast shadows, visible thick extrusion, neon, luminous blue backdrop, glowing edges, rainbow effects, chrome, Apple hardware, Apple logo, house symbol, multiple icons, comparison sheet, labels. SINGLE final app icon only.
```
